Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

ThinkBit Apps ("we," "us," or "our") operates the WakeUpr mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Account information. If you create an account, we collect your email address, and if you sign in with Google or Apple, the name and email address those services share with us. You can also use the App as a guest without creating an account; in that case we store your data under an anonymous identifier only.

Alarm and app-usage data. Alarm schedules, mission types and settings you configure, mission completion history, streaks, coins, and other in-app progress data.

Camera data for mission verification. Some missions (for example, photographing an object or verifying an exercise) use your device's camera. Photos are analyzed to confirm you completed the mission — using on-device processing when possible, or a third-party AI vision service when needed (see Section 3). We do not store the photos themselves — only the pass/fail result of the check is saved to your account.

Purchase information. If you subscribe, purchase and subscription-status information (such as plan type and renewal date) is provided to us by Apple/Google and our subscription-management provider. We never see or store your payment card details — those are handled entirely by Apple's or Google's billing systems.

Device and diagnostic information. Basic device information (device model, OS version) and crash/error logs, used to keep the App working reliably.

2. How we use your information

  • To operate the App's core features: scheduling and firing alarms, verifying missions, and tracking your streaks and progress.
  • To manage your account and, if applicable, your subscription.
  • To send you notifications related to your alarms and app activity.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the App, including diagnosing technical issues.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-party services we use

We rely on the following third-party service providers to operate the App. Each processes certain data on our behalf, under their own privacy and security practices:

  • Supabase — account authentication and database storage for your app data.
  • Google Sign-In / Sign in with Apple — optional social sign-in, if you choose to use it.
  • RevenueCat and Superwall — subscription management and paywall presentation.
  • Together.ai — a cloud AI vision service used as a fallback to verify mission photos when on-device verification isn't available. Photos sent for this check are processed transiently and are not retained by us.
  • Apple and Google — app distribution, push notifications, and in-app billing.

4. Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, contact us at thinkbitapps@gmail.com and we will process your request within a reasonable time.

5. Your choices

  • You can use the App as a guest without providing an email address.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your Apple ID or Google Play account settings.
  • You can disable camera or notification permissions at any time in your device settings, though doing so may limit some App features.

6. Children's privacy

WakeUpr is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Data security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections and access controls on our backend infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International users

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR (EEA/UK) or the CCPA (California). This includes rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do, and for material changes we'll provide additional notice in the App where appropriate.

10. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at thinkbitapps@gmail.com.
